## What NOT to Do

- No background panels behind captions, colored caption words, or overhead headlines.
- No yellow progress bar at the bottom of the frame.
- No solid red borders; use a feathered glow for panic.
- No “paham kan” or FINISH text, and no replacement of the user's narration.
- No smooth morphing between photographic poses; use intentional stop-motion cuts.
- Preserve face visibility and safe margins; keep sound effects below narration during speech.
- No channel branding, source cards, or music-credit cards burned into the video; publish the music attribution in the YouTube description.
- No network dependencies during rendering, infinite animation, or nondeterministic motion.
